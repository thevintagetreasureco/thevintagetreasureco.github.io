About The Vintage Treasure Company

A small, one person shop in Orlando, Florida, specialising in antique and vintage porcelain, fine china and collectible crystal.

The Vintage Treasure Company started the way most porcelain collections do: with a few pieces that were too good to leave behind at an estate sale. It has grown into a curated inventory of more than four hundred pieces, weighted towards French Limoges and Haviland, English bone china teacups, Wedgwood, Japanese Satsuma and Noritake, and retired Swarovski Silver Crystal.

What we do differently is simple. Every piece is photographed in daylight from the front, the back and the mark. Every piece is inspected under a strong lamp for chips, hairlines, crazing, repairs and gilt wear, and anything found is described in the listing and shown in a photograph, not hidden in a phrase like "age appropriate wear." Measurements are taken with a ruler, not estimated. Where a mark is unclear or an attribution is uncertain, the listing says so.

Sales are completed on Etsy, where we hold a 5.0 rating across more than eighty reviews and are a Star Seller. Etsy handles payment and buyer protection; we handle the packing, which means double boxing and individually wrapped pieces, with a 3 to 5 day processing window so nothing is rushed out the door.

If you collect a particular pattern or maker and do not see it here, send us a note. New estate lots arrive regularly and we are happy to keep an eye out.
