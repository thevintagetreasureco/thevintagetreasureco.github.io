Shipping and returns

All orders are placed and paid for on Etsy, so Etsy's purchase protection applies to every sale. These are the shop policies that sit on top of that.

Processing time

Orders ship within 3 to 5 business days. Porcelain is packed by hand, each piece individually wrapped and double boxed, and we would rather take an extra day than ship a rushed parcel.

Shipping

We ship from Orlando, Florida via USPS and UPS with tracking on every order. Shipping cost is calculated on the Etsy listing by weight and destination. If you are buying several pieces, message us before paying and we will combine shipping where it is safe to do so.

Damage in transit

It is rare, but it happens. If a piece arrives damaged, photograph the box, the packing and the piece before discarding anything and message us within 48 hours. We will resolve it through Etsy with a refund or replacement where one exists.

Returns

Please see the current returns and exchanges terms on our Etsy shop policies page, which governs. Condition is described and photographed in detail on every listing so that returns are seldom necessary; if a listing was inaccurate, we will make it right.

International orders

We ship internationally through Etsy where the listing allows it. Duties and import taxes are the buyer's responsibility.
