Contact

Questions about a piece, a pattern you are hunting for, or combined shipping on several items.

The quickest way to reach us is through Etsy Messages, which keeps the conversation attached to your order if you decide to buy.

Message us on Etsy

Before you write

  • Condition questions: every listing has close up photographs of the mark and any flaws. If something is not shown, ask and we will photograph it.
  • Combined shipping: add the items to your Etsy cart and message us before paying; we will adjust shipping where it is safe to pack pieces together.
  • Pattern requests: tell us the maker and pattern name (or send a photograph of the backstamp) and we will let you know if a matching piece comes in.
  • Selling to us: we buy porcelain and Swarovski collections in central Florida. Send photographs of the marks and the group as a whole.

We are in Orlando, Florida and generally reply within one business day.
