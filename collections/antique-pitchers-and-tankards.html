Pitchers & Tankards

Hand painted Limoges cider pitchers and tankards were a favourite project of American china painters at the turn of the twentieth century, which is why so many carry fruit, grapes, currants and dragon handles. This group includes signed J.P.L. and W.G. & Co. blanks, matched tankard and mug sets, and Japanese and Bavarian pitchers. They are decorative first, but most are sound enough to use for water or flowers.
