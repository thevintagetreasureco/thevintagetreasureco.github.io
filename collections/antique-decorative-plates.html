Decorative Plates

This is the largest part of the collection: more than a hundred hand painted and transfer decorated plates from Limoges factories such as Haviland, T&V, J.P.L., B. Luc, Blakeman & Henderson and Coronet, alongside Bavarian, Austrian, Bohemian and English makers. Many are artist signed cabinet plates from the 1890s to 1930s with scalloped gold rims, roses, poppies, fruit and portrait subjects.

Decorative plates are the easiest way to start collecting antique porcelain: they display well on a plate stand or wall rail, they are inexpensive to ship, and the backstamps make them straightforward to date. Every listing photographs the mark.
