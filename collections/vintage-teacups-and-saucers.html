Teacups & Saucers

Every cup and saucer here was chosen for its pattern, its maker and its condition. You will find English bone china from Aynsley, Paragon, Royal Albert, Windsor, Elizabethan and Rosina, French Limoges demitasse cups, German Bavarian sets and hand painted Japanese porcelain. Most sets are single cup and saucer pairs, which makes them easy to add to a mismatched tea service, to give as a gift, or to fill a gap in a pattern you already collect.

Each listing on Etsy shows the backstamp, measurements and any wear to the gilt so you know exactly what you are buying. Click any piece to see full photographs and purchase securely through Etsy.
