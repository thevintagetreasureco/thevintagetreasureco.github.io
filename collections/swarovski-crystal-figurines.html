Swarovski Crystal

Swarovski Silver Crystal figurines from the late 1980s through the 2000s, including retired animals from the Woodland Friends, Barnyard Friends and In a Summer Meadow series, the 1995 Centenary Swan, Crystal Memories gold plated miniatures and SCS designer pieces by Anton Hirzinger, Gabriele Stamey, Max Schreck and Adi Stocker. Where the original box and certificate are present, the listing says so and shows them.
